# Hosting

## How to Host your Discord Bot Engine bot using Project Moyai

Follow these steps to connect your Project Moai server and upload your project files easily.

#### 1. Access the Panel

Once you have purchased your server from the Project Moai Discord:

* Navigate to: [ptero.danicdn.tech](https://ptero.danicdn.tech/)
* **Log in** using the email and password sent to you by the bot or dani in [Project Moyai](https://discord.gg/gydxmBywZd).
* Click on your **Server** to open the management console.

#### 2. Locate SFTP Credentials

You need your specific connection details. Follow the path for your device:

* **On PC:** Look at the left sidebar and click **Settings**.
* **On Mobile:** Tap the hamburger icon (☰) at the top right, then select **Settings**.

Once there, look for the **SFTP Details** section. You will need to copy:

* **Server Address** (include the port, e.g., `sftp://wings.danicdn.tech:2022`)
* **Username**

> &#x20;**Important:** The password for SFTP is the **same password** you used to log in to the web panel.

#### 3. Configure Discord Bot Engine & Upload

Now, switch over to **Discord Bot Engine**:

1. Fill in the required values using the **SFTP Server Address**, **SFTP Username**, and **SFTP Password** you just retrieved.
2. Once connected, go to the editor menu.
3. Click **Project** -> **Upload Project Files**.

**Done!** Your files are now uploading to the server.
